## 1. Executive Concept & Structural Thesis

This video breaks down the **DeepSeek market shock of January 27, 2025**, investigating whether the generative AI industry is an over-leveraged financial bubble or a foundational infrastructure revolution akin to the Dot-Com crash of 2000.

### The 6-Chapter Narrative Arc
1. **Ch 01 · এক দিনে উধাও (00:00 – 00:53):** The shockwave — Nvidia loses $589 Billion in a single day, triggered not by war or pandemic, but by a small Chinese lab giving away state-of-the-art AI for free.
2. **Ch 02 · বড় বাজি (00:54 – 02:14):** Big Tech’s playbook — Silicon Valley borrowing hundreds of billions to build massive data centers under the assumption of an unbreachable "Moat" (pricing power via massive compute).
3. **Ch 03 · ছোট্ট ল্যাবের ধাক্কা (02:15 – 03:32):** DeepSeek’s breakthrough — How US chip bans forced algorithmic frugality (R1 rivaling OpenAI o1 at 1/20th to 1/50th the inference cost) and open-weight distribution.
4. **Ch 04 · টাকা পুড়ছে কোথায় (03:33 – 05:25):** The Economics — Training cost vs. inference profit (the Fuchka stall analogy), open-source efficiency, commodity pricing, and why speculative debt creates an asset bubble.
5. **Ch 05 · অন্য দিকটা (05:26 – 06:09):** The Counter-Perspective — Jevons Paradox (cheaper tokens = exponentially higher volume), frontier model moats, and the reality check on DeepSeek’s self-reported figures.
6. **Ch 06 · আমাদের কী & সমাপনী (06:10 – 07:51):** Global South & Bangladesh impact — Data sovereignty, local server hosting, and the 2000 Dot-com telecom cable historical parallel.

### Casting & Anatomy
* **Protagonist (The Host):** Wears a solid vermilion (`#F53816`) long-sleeve top and charcoal trousers. Stiff two-leg pendulum walk cycle; arms bend at sharp $45^\circ$ angles when checking his smartphone.
* **Extras / Tech Workers / Crowds:** Monochromatic desaturated slate (`#6F7388` / `#3A3D4D`).
* **The Signature Glitch Visor:** Every human figure (and anthropomorphic robot) has a horizontal electric-blue (`#2638FF`) rectangle across eye level with a 2px offset tab, creating an anonymous, cybernetic journalistic tone.

### Surface & Texture Engine
* **Screen-Print Misregistration:** 2–4px horizontal color offset between linework and fills (seen on the Bangladesh map and chapter placards).
* **Ben-Day Halftone Dot Grids:** Procedural circular raster patterns applied to:
  * Lower half of the Vermilion Sun disc.
  * Glowing halo fields around the blue AI orb and smartphone screens.
  * Deep-sea trenches in the transatlantic cable sequence.
  * Transitional screen wipes (halftone dot sweeps).
* **Technical Production Chrome:**
  * **Corner Crosshairs (`+`):** Positioned at $(24, 24)$, $(W-24, 24)$, $(24, H-24)$, and $(W-24, H-24)$.
  * **Top Progress Bar:** 3px thick vermilion bar tracking global timeline progress $(0 \to W)$.
  * **Top-Left Chapter Pill:** Dark container with a white numeric box (`০১`, `০২`, etc.) and medium Bengali sans chapter title.
  * **Bottom-Left Swatch Matrix:** 4 square color chips (`#F53816`, `#2638FF`, `#6F7388`, `#10121A`).
  * **Bottom-Right Monospace Stamp:** `EP.02 · NN` in monospace.
  * **Subtitle Container:** Solid black capsule with a 4px vermilion vertical bar on its left edge.

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Parametric Animation Directives

To replicate this production in an animation framework (After Effects, Remotion, Canvas, or Motion Canvas), apply the following rules:

### Coordinate Space & Camera Rigging
* **Canvas Resolution:** $1920 \times 1080$ (16:9), centered around $(960, 540)$.
* **Camera Rig:** Do not use continuous panning. The visual grammar uses **Discrete Camera Anchors**:
  * `Wide Street Anchor`: $X: 960, Y: 540$, Zoom: $1.0\times$.
  * `Phone Push-in Anchor`: $X: 420, Y: 520$, Zoom: $1.45\times$.
  * `Skyline Overhead`: $X: 960, Y: 320$, Zoom: $0.9\times$.
* **Camera Moves:** Apply cubic easing (`eInOutCubic`) over 18 to 24 frames ($0.6\text{s} - 0.8\text{s}$), holding static between transitions.

### Layer Hierarchy (Bottom to Top)
1. **Background Layer (`z: 0`):** Paper Cream (`#ECE6D5`) fill with seeded noise flecks and vignette.
2. **Atmosphere / Silhouettes (`z: 10`):** Dhaka architecture, minarets, transmission wires, and the halftone sun.
3. **Primary Set & Props (`z: 20`):** Street pavement, tea stall, server racks, bank facades, and conference desks.
4. **Character Layer (`z: 30`):** Protagonist and crowd extras. Footings anchored to the ground line ($Y = 820$).
5. **Callout & Infographic Overlay (`z: 40`):** Charts, speech bubbles, balance scale, watermelon carts, and floating data cards.
6. **Annotation Stamps (`z: 50`):** Angled red outline rubber stamps rotated between $-4^\circ$ and $-8^\circ$ entering with an elastic snap (`eOutBack`).
7. **Fixed Technical Chrome (`z: 100`):** Corner crosshairs, progress bar, chapter pill, swatch matrix, and subtitle capsule.

### Animation Curve Standards
* **Data Counter Ingestion:** Integer incrementation using `floor(lerp(startVal, endVal, eOutExpo(u)))`.
* **Stamp Impact:**
  $$\text{Scale}(t) = \text{clamp}\left(1.0 + 0.35 \cdot (1 - u)^2 \cdot \cos(u \cdot 12), 0, 1.35\right)$$
* **Halftone Pulsing:** Oscillate radius by $\pm 3.5\text{px}$ using a sine phase: $\sin(t \cdot 6.28)$.
