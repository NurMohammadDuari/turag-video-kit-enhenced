Character and scene atlas

The cast uses the supplied reference proportions, clothing, hair, profile heads, short necks, shoes and pose rules. Scenes are editable constructions from the reference; they are not recovered original source code.

TuragCharacters.draw(context, options) builds each pose. TuragScenes.draw(context, seconds, beat) draws each scene at a deterministic time. The episode player adds captions, chapter tags and progress.
