# Validation and evidence checklist

A render must be complete, readable, aligned and reproducible. A valid MP4 container does not prove those properties.

## Input and timing

- Probe the actual supplied audio and video: duration, streams, fps, dimensions and decodability.
- Record where transcript text and timestamps came from. Keep `timing.verified=false` for estimated/supplied-but-unchecked alignment.
- Preserve names, numbers, units and uncertainty. Source claims in the supplied script are not automatically independently verified.
- Ensure cues are ordered and in bounds; scenes cover the full intended timeline. Flag negative lengths, accidental overlaps and unsupported IDs.
- Check chapter starts, a mid-episode passage and the ending against narration. Silence detection is not transcription or word alignment.

## Picture and typography

- Wait for all local fonts before first frame; ensure preview/render needs no external font server.
- Inspect Bengali conjuncts and mixed Bengali/Latin text at output resolution.
- Measure captions and labels; no cut-off text, tiny auto-shrunk paragraphs, or subtitles over essential action.
- Confirm proper safe margins, chapter labels, progress rule and scene counter.
- Inspect every distinct scene family and each difficult transition. Whole-video contact sheets check coverage; full-size frames check anatomy/type.
- Compare night office, street, doctors, rice field, garment, government, bank and ending with reference anchors.
- Character silhouettes must remain long and thin, with small heads, short necks, narrow joints and grounded shoes.
- Count literal grids; confirm bars/percentages/data labels agree. An illustrative grid must not imply an unsupported exact value.
- Check first and final visible frames and chapter boundaries for black flashes, missing layers or premature exit.

## Determinism and rendering

- Seek t, then another time, then t again: the image should match.
- Keep random seeds stable. No accumulating animation state, `Math.random`, current clock, or render-speed dependence.
- Run a short smoke encode before the full render. Verify ffmpeg exit status and reject missing/empty output.
- Inspect full output metadata: video and audio present, intended dimensions/fps, expected duration within a frame/audio-codec tolerance.
- Decode the finished output to detect corrupt frames. A filename alone is not evidence of success.
- For a copied-reference output, validate picture identity separately from the newly encoded audio; do not imply reconstruction.

## Audio

- Confirm correct input file, start alignment, full narration and intelligible levels.
- Keep added effects optional and quiet; inspect clipping and loud transitions.
- Account for MP3 encoder delay and small metadata-duration differences. Do not stretch narration to conceal a timing mismatch.
- Check the final second and tail; a source card may continue while narration finishes, but speech should not be cut.

## Evidence levels

| Statement | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| “Every source frame was decoded” | Decoder count and per-frame records reaching the final PTS |
| “Every source frame has an index entry” | Frame-number/PTS index covering all decoded frames |
| “The whole video was visually reviewed” | Chronological contact sheets or playback coverage; state sampling interval |
| “Every frame was visually inspected” | Actual visual inspection of every frame; a metric scan is insufficient |
| “Timing is verified” | Listening/alignment review against narration, not merely copied timestamps |
| “Exact reference picture” | Copy/remux provenance and matching decoded-frame evidence |
| “New scene matches the grammar” | Comparison against nearest reference and explicit design review |
| “Pixel-exact regenerated clone” | Frame-level comparison demonstrating it; do not make this claim for the template rebuild |

## Delivery report

Record source paths/hashes, output paths, durations/frame counts, commands used, validation results and remaining differences. Include the editable manifest, shared engine and fonts. Describe the reference-with-audio and code-rebuilt outputs separately. If an output is approximate, say what is approximate: timing, character shape, motion, scene composition, or all of these.

The current project ships audit artifacts under `reference/analysis/` and generated outputs under `output/`. Use actual current files and results in the final report; do not mark a checklist complete just because this document exists.
