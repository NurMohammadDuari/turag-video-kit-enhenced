## Character and prop lessons

Characters are narrow silhouettes, with a small profile head and visible neck. A raised phone is tiny relative to the person. Arm segments bend at an elbow and remain attached. Hair and clothing distinguish extras; a recurring person does not change hair between cuts. Cream panjabi, long dress with red hem, short cap, long hair and bun are observed variants. A blue strip is not mandatory on every face.

Furniture is thinner than body masses: chair strokes, desk top, monitor stem and rails. A seated person's hip, thigh, knee and shoe must meet the furniture and floor. Most props belong to a workplace, street, surface or deliberate diagram. Real environments carry supporting detail: window mullions, fans, plants, files, wires, awnings, cups and machinery. Removing all such detail produces a sparse approximation, even if the colors match.

A new profession should reuse the rig, clothing logic and furniture scale, with a specific tool and action. A new machine should use flat mechanical masses, small hinges/slots and the established accent colors. Avoid changing to glossy 3D, rounded app icons, thick black character outlines or oversized cartoon heads.

## Composition and motion lessons

The picture often changes through a reveal or transformation within one location. Reusing the backdrop is intentional; repeating a frozen unrelated object arrangement is not. Show the transition from initial state to consequence. Cameras pan between neighboring rooms, zoom from grid to one worker, or return from a detail to a wider explanation. New camera keyframes support those decisions while keeping subtitles and chrome outside world transforms.

Use negative space purposefully: broad quiet sky above a grounded street, evidence panel above workers, or one large diagram beside a small guide. The source is not uniformly minimal; some scenes require rows, a dense grid, or layered city detail. Match the density of the selected reference family.

Halftone is structured: a screen halo, lower sun edge, field or wipe edge. Paper has soft tone variation and fibres. Repeating blur/glow on all props does not reproduce it. Cards have hard offsets; captions sit tightly in near-black rectangles with a thin vermilion edge.

## For an unseen scene

Example: a new script line about delivery dispatch is not in these frames. Borrow the office/street scale and dashed-route grammar, draw a dispatcher at a real desk, add recognizable flat delivery vehicles and a route display, then animate assignment of a route. Do not substitute the hospital or a generic AI orb merely because dispatch is missing from the prop registry.

The AI chooses the image and draws the missing props. `style-lock.json` limits aesthetic drift; `08_DIRECTOR_RULES.md` defines the decisions and checks. Inspect the completed shot against its selected evidence before marking the plan authored. These resources support consistent reconstruction; they do not train a model or guarantee a pixel-identical unseen shot.
